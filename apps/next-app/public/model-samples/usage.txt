Pixal3D sample library: free evaluation usage

These original reference drawings and the new models generated from them are
provided for personal, educational and non-commercial evaluation. You may
download them, inspect or modify them, and import them into a non-commercial
test scene without registering an account or paying a fee.

Commercial use or redistribution of the sample files is not granted by this
usage note. A separate decision from the site operator is needed for that use.
The samples do not include rigging or animation and are not certified for
printing or production use. Geometry-only STL/OBJ conversions have no textures.

This note applies only to the files explicitly listed in this site's new sample
library. It does not apply to third-party examples or community-gallery assets.
The MIT license of the inference code is separate from this sample usage note.

Provenance: see each sample's generation.json and geometry-exports.json beside
the files. Original references: scripts/model-samples/create-references.mjs.
