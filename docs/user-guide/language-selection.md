# Choosing your language

Pixal3D is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

On your first visit, the site normally follows your browser's preferred language. If the browser does not request English or Chinese, the site may use the request's country information as a fallback. English remains the final default.

Use the language control in the header to switch between **English** and **简体中文**. The control is available before and after sign-in. Your manual choice is remembered for one year and takes priority on later visits. Switching language keeps the current page and its query parameters whenever that page exists in both languages.

English pages use clean addresses such as `/blog`; Simplified Chinese pages use `/zh-CN/blog`. Sharing either address opens that explicit language version regardless of the recipient's automatic language setting.
