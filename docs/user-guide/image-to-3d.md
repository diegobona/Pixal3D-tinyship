# Choosing a model and downloading a file

`/image-to-3d-model-free-download` provides actual self-hosted sample files without a Pixal3D account. The current mushroom sample has a textured GLB and geometry-only OBJ/STL conversions. Its displayed picture is the original input illustration. Open the downloaded model in your target application to inspect the generated geometry and material.

Downloads are free for personal, educational and non-commercial evaluation under the linked sample usage note. Commercial reuse is not currently granted. Your own generated assets also depend on input-image rights and the model/service terms.

GLB packages the exported material with the model. The supplied OBJ/STL files have no textures. STL does not certify printability: check units, closed surfaces and wall thickness in a slicer. FBX appears in the format guide but has no download button because no FBX export is provided.

`/image-to-3d` compares named releases of Pixal3D, Hyper3D Rodin, Microsoft TRELLIS and Tencent Hunyuan3D. Each card explains a reason to try it, output formats, running conditions and the scope of published performance figures. The card links lead to official sources. Published figures from different hardware and settings do not predict a common speed ranking.

Both pages embed the existing Pixal3D community Space as a provisional workspace. The embed does not switch among the four models. It loads near the viewport and supplies reload/external-open help. A provider queue, quota, login requirement or outage can affect generation without affecting the site's self-hosted sample downloads.

English pages have clean URLs; Chinese pages use `/zh-CN`. Related links connect the two pages to local installation, GGUF, low-VRAM and ComfyUI tutorials.

## Requesting a tool

The homepage feedback form asks what you need from 3D modeling software, an online service or an AI tool. Describe the part of your workflow that is difficult and what a tool should do for you, such as repairing mesh errors or processing models in batches. You can respond in any language, up to 3,000 characters.
